A new video game appears on the horizon, and its name is <a href="https://rorasuketo.win">Rorasuketo</a>! My friend <a href="http://dontcallmecandy.com">Cam</a> deigned to become a game developer many years ago, and despite time, various setbacks, and the fickle nature of RPGMaker, that deignment became a reality, and Rorasuketo can now be bought and installed from Steam, which makes it a Real Game(tm), in this day and age. The name comes from the Japanese transliteration of "roller skate", and it's fun to say.

To quote from the <a href="https://rorasuketo.win">official website</a>:
<blockquote>
Rorasuketo is a ghost sports adventure created and designed by Cam Norcross, and co-written by Tiffany VanPeenen. Inspired by and orchestrated with classic SNES sounds, the soundtrack was written by Michael Chadwick.
</blockquote>

You may have noticed that last name is the same as mine. Yes, I created the soundtrack that plays in the background during the game, and I was glad to do it. I found a bunch of soundfonts at some point that included sampled instruments from popular SNES video games, and used them to arrange my own compositions, to really give the game that SNES-ified flavor.

It is a wonderful accomplishment, and many many hours of effort went into crafting the visuals, characters, story, and mini-games, and you should go play it.

Note: the podcast I still do bi-weekly with my friend Matt did <a href="https://hackingthegrepson.podbean.com/e/hacking-the-grepson-115-rorasukeo-creator-interview/">an interview with Cam</a> about the game a bit ago.

EDIT (2026-10-07): Finished my first full playthough, and I adore it even more. What an incredibly personal and effecting story. Nice job, Cam.
