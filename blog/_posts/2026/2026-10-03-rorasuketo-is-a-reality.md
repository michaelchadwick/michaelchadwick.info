A new video game appears on the horizon, and its name is <a href="https://rorasuketo.win">Rorasuketo</a>! My friend Cam deigned to become a game developer many years ago, and despite time, various setbacks, and the fickle nature of RPGMaker, that deignment became a reality, and Rorasuketo can now be bought and installed from Steam, which makes it a Real Game(tm), in this day and age. The name comes from the Japanese transliteration of "roller skate", and it's fun to say.

To quote from the <a href="https://rorasuketo.win">official website</a>:
<blockquote>
Rorasuketo is a ghost sports adventure being created and designed by Cam Norcross, and co-written by Tiffany VanPeenen. Inspired by and orchestrated with classic SNES sounds, the soundtrack was written by Michael Chadwick.
</blockquote>

You may have noticed that last name is the same as mine. Yes, I created the soundtrack that plays in the background during the game, and I was glad to do it. I found a bunch of soundfonts at some point that included sampled instruments from popular SNES video games, and used them to arrange my own compositions, to really give the game that SNES-ified flavor.

It is a wonderful accomplishment, and many many hours of effort went into crafting the visuals and the characters and the story and the mini-games, and you should go play it.
